# Video Production QA

Verify evidence, not confidence.

Gate order:
1. source/authorship revision;
2. structural scene/composition consistency;
3. motion/timing across representative frames and transitions;
4. visual frames/contact sheet at intended aspect ratio;
5. audio presence/sync/levels when applicable;
6. delivery metadata and play-through;
7. reproducibility source/scripts and known limitations.

For Blender, do not pass solely from a screenshot. Inspect object/scene/animation/render state and sample the frames that matter.

When a gate fails, report the smallest reproducible failure, affected stage, evidence, and next bounded fix. When the skill stack itself changes, record the experiment result and the skill change implied by evidence.
